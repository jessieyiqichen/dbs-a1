How I work

Principles
  • Pain point before solution.
  • Staged rollout and attribution.
  • Define the metric before the number.
  • Cost is a product parameter.

Pain point before solution. Confirm which layer the bottleneck lives in before building. Get the layer wrong and no feature fixes it.

Staged rollout and attribution. One layer of change per stage, so a problem points at its layer immediately. Multimodal retrieval shipped in three stages, merged in two days, zero rollbacks.

Define the metric before the number. Any number in a report must name its dashboard, its metric and its read date.

Cost is a product parameter. Every model call logged and routed by task tier; a shadow run proves quality holds before cost is cut. Savings are the result of verification, not the goal.
