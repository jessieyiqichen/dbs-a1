How I work

  • Pain point before solution. Confirm which layer the bottleneck lives in before building. For classroom questions it is input friction, not model capability; for bookmarks it is the act of having to start.
  • Staged rollout and attribution. One layer of change per stage, so a problem points at its layer. Multimodal retrieval shipped in three stages, merged in two days, zero rollbacks.
  • Define the metric before the number. Marks reviewed after class, not marks made in class. Daily actives by UV, not PV.
  • Cost is a product parameter. Every model call is logged and routed by tier. A shadow run proves quality holds before cost is cut.
