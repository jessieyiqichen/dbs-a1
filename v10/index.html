Product judgment is proven by products that ship. This is how I work.

  • Pain point before solution. Before building, confirm which layer the bottleneck lives in: for classroom questions it is input friction, not model capability; for bookmark organizing it is the act of "having to start" itself.
  • Staged rollout and attribution. Ship in stages, one layer of change per stage, so a problem points at its layer immediately. Multimodal retrieval shipped in three stages, fully merged in two days, zero rollbacks.
  • Define the metric before the number. Marks reviewed after class, not marks made in class. Daily actives by UV, not PV. Any number in a report must name its dashboard, its metric and its read date.
  • Cost is a product parameter. Every model call is logged and routed to a model tier by task. Before cutting cost, a shadow run proves quality holds. Savings are the result of verification, not the goal.
