Products, shipped solo

Lecture Companion

Real-time lecture AI assistant · solo build

The bottleneck for asking questions in class is input friction, not model capability. I compressed a question into one click on a transcript sentence and used multimodal retrieval to fill in whiteboard and formulas. North Star: after-class review.

63 marks, 59 with zero typing · 3-stage rollout, zero rollbacks · ASR cost cut to ¼

xhs-threads

Thought threads from Xiaohongshu bookmarks · solo build

Bookmarking is frequent; organizing almost never happens. The user only saves; the AI threads, annotates and checks. Positioning from a survey of four competitor types; an independent reviewer model gates output quality.

11 threads, 63 nodes (2026-09) · 90% of calls on the light tier · ¥0.05–0.10 per item

Nous

Cognitive-twin message agent · three clients

Other bots learn how you talk; this one learns how you decide. A nine-dimension cognitive model judges whether to reply, drafts in your way, and warns before you say yes to something you will not do. Feishu, Mac and Web, in daily use.

Behavior prediction 49% → 71% · 3 clients live · correction flywheel closed

JobPilot

AI job-search strategist · solo build · open source

The job search is blocked by people, not information. JobPilot plugs a cognitive model and language samples into personal diagnosis and messaging that sounds like you. All six product trade-offs have a written why.

349 jobs collected, 37 high-score · 31 CLI commands, 544 tests (2026-09) · CI + live demo

MusiClaw

Shanghai musical-theatre market data · solo build

Public ticketing data turned into a data product that updates itself daily: seat-map snapshots, cast attribution, four content pipelines feeding a real data account. The AI's scope of responsibility is written down.

86 shows snapshotted daily · 4 content pipelines · fixed 24 h post-publish review
