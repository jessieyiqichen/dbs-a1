4How I work

Pain point before solution.
Confirm which layer the bottleneck lives in before building. For classroom questions it is input friction, not model capability; for bookmarks it is the act of having to start. Get the layer wrong and no feature fixes it.
Staged rollout and attribution.
One layer of change per stage, so a problem points at its layer immediately. Multimodal retrieval shipped in three stages, merged in two days, zero rollbacks.
Define the metric before the number.
A North Star that reflects real value: marks reviewed after class, not marks made in class. Daily actives by UV, not PV. Any number in a report names its dashboard, metric and read date.
Cost is a product parameter.
Every model call logged and routed by task tier. A shadow run proves quality holds before cost is cut. Savings are the result of verification, not the goal.
