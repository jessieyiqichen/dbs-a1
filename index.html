Design, Build, Ship · Assignment 1 · Progress 2 / 25

One personal landing page, 25 versions

One idea, content frozen, only the form changes. The visitor is a hiring manager for an AI product role. After reading they should understand how I think about and ship products, then open one project or send an email. v01–v10 go wide, v11–v18 narrow to two or three lines and combine them, v19–v25 refine one line only.

Starting point I already have a live personal site (jessieyiqichen.github.io): paper background, serif headings, numbered sections, five projects laid out as evidence. This assignment questions whether that form is right. It is one candidate, not the answer.

Act 1 · Go wide v01–v10 · each version changes one axis: layout / audience / mood / era / medium metaphor / visitor action

v01
Interactive résumé + guide
A CV written in prose on grey paper, Source Serif 4 for everything, one blue. Every entry opens to its reason, a screenshot and links; my own memoji offers a seven-stop tour and walks beside each entry. Restyled after a reference I picked (Monica Sanchez on One Page Love): the first sans-serif, white-sheet version read as AI output. Two earlier v01s (a ledger, then a plain single column) were dropped: one stopped reading as a person, the other gave the visitor nothing to do.
v02
Swiss grid
Black, white, one red. A 12-column grid, 6px rules, the name at poster size, three giant numbers, screenshots locked to the grid. Style comes from typography and structure, not decoration. Two earlier v02s were dropped: a fake "live transcript" (form was not real) and a generic showcase (no point of view).
